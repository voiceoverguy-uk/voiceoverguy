Patrick Stewart Voiceover for the 2021 European Collaboration Summit

This is actually my third voiceover for the European Collaboration Summit. In 2018 I employed a soft version of my Patrick Stewart voice for the European Collaboration Summit's promo for their conference in Mainz, while in 2020 they went with a completely different style and asked me to use my professional narrator voice. In 2021, the marketing team at the European Collaboration Summit put together a wonderfully inspiring promo video celebrating community and my classic Patrick Stewart voice felt just right for relaying the message. Among my most popular voices, I've used the Patrick Stewart voice for many promotions, such as my work for Minecraft and the NFL.

About the European Collaboration Summit 2021

The European Collaboration Summit is a major European modern workplace event about everything related to office work. 2021's conference was all about championing inclusion. The message was loud and clear: all are welcome at the European Collaboration Summit! In fact, it's the very diversity of the event that makes it such a success. This is my third piece of work for the summit and I'm continually impressed by the content they put out across media platforms to tie in with their events. When I saw the theme of this year's meeting in Dusseldorf, I knew my classic Patrick Stewart voice would work in harmony with the script.
