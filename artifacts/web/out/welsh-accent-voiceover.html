Looking for a Welsh Accent?

A genuine Welsh accent available from a Welsh born and bred professional voiceover

February 2019

The Welsh Accent

Whilst I like to pride myself on my wide variety of styles and accents, I do know my limitations and am more than happy to recommend the right person for the job, rather than me provide a sub standard product. Welsh is one of them. My associate, PJ was born and grew up in Wales, so it's a no brainer when I have enquires, I recommend PJ. Have a listen to the demo and I'm confident you will be more than happy.


More about PJ

Paul Jones (PJ) is an established British voice-actor born and raised in the South Wales valleys.
As well as a neutral RP he can call upon his native Welsh accent; the tone of which can be tailored to suit a wide range of projects.

Get in Touch!

If you're working on a commercial or unique product or project and you need a Welsh accent, then please feel free to get in touch. Both myself and PJ provide countless voiceovers for global projects, so browse through my site for more examples of my work or for Welsh, I'll happily pass you to PJ. To make even easier if you'd prefer, we can provide you with a free voiceover sample from your script!
