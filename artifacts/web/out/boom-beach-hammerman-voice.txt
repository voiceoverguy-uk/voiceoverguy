3:T515,<h2>Who is Hammerman in Boom Beach?</h2>
<p>Hammerman is the blackguard commander and one of the key villains in Boom Beach. With a blocky square nose, a battle-scarred left eye, and a military-grade scowl, he’s not exactly what you’d call approachable.</p>

<p>Hammerman is known for launching full-scale attacks from multiple enemy bases scattered across the archipelago, dubbed “Hammerman’s HQ.” He’s the thorn in every player’s side, turning up uninvited and smashing your base to bits.</p>

<p>If you fancy diving deeper into his evil escapades, you’ll find a full breakdown on <a href="/https://boombeach.fandom.com/wiki/Lt._Hammerman" target="_blank" title="Hammerman Boom Beach Fandom">Boom Beach Fandom</a> and on <a href="/https://supercell.com/en/games/boombeach/" target="_blank" title="Official Boom Beach site">Supercell’s official site</a>.</p>

<p>I’ve voiced Hammerman in several versions over the years, including the gloriously bizarre <a href="/boom-beach-hammerman-hot-tub-voice" title="Hammerman’s Hot Tub Voice">Hot Tub edition</a>. Yep, he’s a villain who apparently enjoys a soak. Honestly, voicing characters like this is what makes <a href="/character-voiceover" title="Character voiceover work">character voiceover work</a> so much fun.</p>
