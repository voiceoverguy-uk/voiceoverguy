My Attenborough Voice Gets Back to Nature

Anyone who’s spent more than 30 seconds on my site knows I’ve a rather popular David Attenborough impression. From epic game trailers like Horizon Zero Dawn to viral airline parodies, I get asked to voice all sorts of wild and wonderful scripts in the style of Sir David.

So, when the opportunity came up to lend that voice to a nature simulation game, Natural Instincts, it felt like a match made in gaming heaven. The concept was spot on – a nature documentary simulation, teaching players how to protect animals and ecosystems.

If you’d like to experiment with your own scripts, you can try out the free Attenborough Voice Generator and see how your idea might sound!

About Natural Instincts

Natural Instincts is an interactive nature sim game that began life on Kickstarter. The premise: protect the environment, guide animal behaviour, and learn how ecosystems thrive – all delivered in a relaxing gameplay loop.

It’s educational, it’s beautifully animated, and it was an ideal excuse to go full-Attenborough. Whether narrating a baby badger’s struggle or a squirrel’s foraging frenzy, the project made full use of my signature documentary tone.
