Bank Sohar Explainer Voiceover

One of the many explainers I voice each week. A market I know well and I'm trusted by well known brands on a daily basis.

Check out the Bank Sohar App Explainer video

I'm a trusted and well-respected British male voice over. I've spent a lot of time and effort developing my natural and conversational style and, as as result, I get many requests for this kind of voiceover each week. It was great working with the team at Bank Sohar and I think they have a pretty nifty app, so it was easy to do it justice with my voiceover. Since the Apple commercials a few years back, explainer video voiceover is the style I'm asked for most often. It's used on web videos and short promotional videos when the product or service is complicated enough that a normal commercial ad isn't long enough to do it justice.
