September 2020

Professional Voiceover for the 2020 European Collaboration Summit

I've actually provided voiceover for the European Collaboration Summit once before, with my classic Patrick Stewart Voice. So, given how well the last video worked out, I was more than happy to work with them again. They wanted a very different style for this video, but there was still plenty of humour throughout the script, so I had a great time with it. This is my softer, natural voiceover style, and it's a great for this kind of advertisement, but it's especially good for explainer videos where you need the information to be as clear as possible.

A little more about the European Collab Summit 2020

The European Collaboration Summit is a major European modern workplace event about everything related to office work, incluing things like Office 365, Teams, SharePoint, Power Platform, and a huge range of business apps. It's essentially the best place in the world to attend if you're looking for new tools to increase your office's productivity. They put on a new summit each year and it's always well attended. I like the effort they put into their ads and I'm always happy to work with these guys. Check out their website in the link above and find out if the summit is what you've been looking for to take your business to the next level. And, if you need to know how to book a ticket, just watch this video again!
