Create a proper Privacy Policy page for voiceoverguy.co.uk and wire it into the site cleanly.

What to build:
1. Add a new Privacy Policy page in the same visual style as the rest of the site.
2. Add a footer link to the Privacy Policy if one does not already exist.
3. Make sure the page has its own SEO metadata, canonical URL, Open Graph tags, and sensible page title and meta description.
4. Use British English throughout.
5. Do not use em dashes anywhere in the copy.

Policy requirements:
Write a clear, professional Privacy Policy for a UK-based voiceover website. Keep it human, readable, and trustworthy, not overly legalistic, but still complete enough for a small business website.

The policy should cover:
- who operates the site: Guy Harris / VoiceoverGuy
- what personal data may be collected through contact and enquiry forms, such as name, email address, phone number, company name, project details, and message content
- that data is collected only when users choose to submit it
- what the data is used for: replying to enquiries, discussing projects, providing quotes, and customer communication
- that personal data is not sold
- that data is only shared where necessary to run the website or respond to enquiries, for example secure hosting, email delivery, spam protection, or embedded media services
- embedded third-party content such as YouTube videos may place cookies or collect usage data when viewed
- basic analytics and website performance data may be collected, including aggregate usage information, page visits, browser/device data, and IP-related technical logs where relevant
- explain cookies in a sensible, honest way, including essential site functionality and possible third-party cookies from embedded media
- include a section on lawful basis under UK GDPR in plain English, such as legitimate interests and consent where relevant
- include data retention wording saying enquiry information is kept only as long as reasonably necessary for business, legal, or tax purposes
- include data security wording saying reasonable steps are taken to protect submitted information
- include users’ rights under UK data protection law, including access, correction, deletion, and objection where applicable
- include contact details section with enquiries@voiceoverguy.co.uk as the privacy contact unless the codebase already uses a different official address
- include a last updated date at the bottom

Important wording/tone:
- keep it clean, modern, and reassuring
- avoid sounding like a giant corporate legal department
- make it suitable for a small UK creative business
- keep paragraphs short and readable

Also do this:
- if the site has an inline blog enquiry form or contact form, ensure its small privacy note links to the new Privacy Policy page
- where there is a submit button on enquiry forms, add a subtle line beneath it such as:
  “By sending this form, you agree that we may use your details to reply to your enquiry. See our Privacy Policy.”
- style that line neatly so it feels premium and consistent with the brand

SEO:
- page title around 55 to 65 characters
- meta description around 150 to 160 characters
- include appropriate schema if helpful, but keep it valid and simple
