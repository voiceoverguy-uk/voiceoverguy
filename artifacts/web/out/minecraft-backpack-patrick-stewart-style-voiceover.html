Another chance to bring out my trusted Patrick Stewart-style voice. Minecraft + backpack + mock seriousness = a perfect recipe for parody narration.

August 2019

Voicing Another Minecraft Promo

I was asked once again to lend my voice to a Minecraft campaign — this time, for a quirky backpack promo. As one of the biggest brands in gaming, Minecraft always brings big expectations, but the creative on this one hit all the right notes.

With a mock scientist angle and dry humour, it felt like the perfect moment to dust off the Patrick Stewart-style voice. The team at Mojang were chuffed — and so was I.

Why Minecraft Still Matters

Launched in 2009, Minecraft continues to dominate the sandbox gaming world — because if you can imagine it, you can build it. This promo was tied to a new merch drop, giving me a chance to mix fun with formality in the narration.

If you're exploring Minecraft for the first time (or the fiftieth), check out the Minecraft official merch shop — or browse Mojang’s channels for more surprises.

As long as they keep making scripts this good, I’ll happily keep voicing Minecraft videos!
