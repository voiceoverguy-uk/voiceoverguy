Spooky Voice - Alton Towers Scarefest

Proud to voice Alton Towers’ Scarefest once again — delivering spooky, spine-tingling voiceovers that echo across their award-winning Halloween mazes.

October 2022

The spooky scary voice of Scarefest at Alton Towers

This really is one of my favourite times of the year. All the Halloween Voices come out to play — and then it rolls straight into Christmas season!

I bet I voice 50+ Halloween projects in the run-up to 31st October. I love this Vincent Price-esque style voice. It’s even better when I hear what awesome producers can do with my evil character voice or spooky laughs!

If you're after something darker still, have a listen to my Ghost Face Scream voice or even the classic Wilhelm Screams.

What is Scarefest?

Scarefest at Alton Towers is one of the UK’s biggest Halloween events. With creepy mazes, award-winning attractions, and new frights every year — it’s a proper scream!

From the eerie Wicker Man to the immersive Alton Towers Dungeon, visitors brave three multi-award-winning scare mazes and twisted rides in pitch darkness. Perfect for spine-tingling October nights. It’s a dream gig for a Halloween voiceover artist like me!

Trusted Voice for Theme Parks & Haunted Attractions

From the terrifying screams of Ghost Face to the eerie calm of evil narrators, I’ve voiced Halloween campaigns for the UK’s biggest theme parks — including Alton Towers and Thorpe Park.

It’s not just about being scary — it’s about creating the right mood. Whether you need unsettling whispers, creepy characters, or full-on screams, I can deliver broadcast-quality audio in any style.
