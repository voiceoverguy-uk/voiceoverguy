Who are Batelco and what is special about their WiFi?


Batelco is the first and most advanced nationwide WiFi digital network in the Kingdom of Bahrain. Bahrain WiFi is a growing network of WiFi spots around Bahrain that help enhance connectivity. Look for Bahrain Wi-Fi icon to connect to Wi-Fi on the go around the kingdom and upgrade the way you communicate.
