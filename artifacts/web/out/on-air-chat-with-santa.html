On-Air Christmas Chat with Santa

Having been the BBC Radio 1 Santa for over 5 years and the BBC Radio 2 Santa over the past couple (along with appearances on CBBC, ITV, and elsewhere), I've garnered a fair bit of experience in character as jolly old Saint Nick. It was not then a surprise when I ended up becoming the on-air voice of Father Christmas over on Santa Radio, the world's best Christmas radio channel. Being the host of Santa Radio is a great deal of fun and it's the perfect place to get into the Christmas spirit! And now YOU can join Santa for an on-air, festive chat!


Santa Radio ~ In the Air ~ On the Air

Santa is also broadcasting 24/7, 365 days of the year from his workshop on the UK's No.1 Christmas radio station. This is non-stop Christmas Radio as it SHOULD be. Featuring shows, celebrity guests and a NEW MUG SHOTS page,
