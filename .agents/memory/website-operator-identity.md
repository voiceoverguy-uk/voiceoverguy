---
name: Website operator identity
description: Privacy-policy identity requires separate confirmation from the voiceover contracting identity.
---

The user wants the privacy-page identity left unchanged until they confirm who legally operates the website and controls its enquiry data. The company named in the voiceover terms is not automatically the site's operator or data controller.

**Why:** A contracting party for voiceover bookings and an operator or controller for website enquiries may be different legal persons. Changing the privacy statement by inference could misidentify the responsible party.

**How to apply:** Before any privacy identity rewrite, obtain the user's explicit confirmation and approval of the exact wording. Publishing or revising booking terms alone is not that confirmation.
