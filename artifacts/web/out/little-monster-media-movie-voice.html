Movie trailer - Little Monster Media

A punchy movie trailer voice with a lot of impact!

August 2018

My movie trailer voice for a great radio imaging company!

I've known and worked with Adam from Little Monster Media for many years, so it's always nice when he gets in touch with another job for me. Little Monster Media needed me to riff on my movie trailer voice while reading their really funny script. Adam wanted to advertise LMM's complete radio imagining and SFX package and I LOVE how quirky and fun the finished product is. You can check out my radio imagine service, if you're interested, and you may like to listen to a few of my other movie trailer voiceovers, such as my Rocket and Groot and Turtle Strike videos!

A little more about Little Monster Media

Little Monster Media is an audio production company based in Bristol. They have years of experience and accolades to their name and htey specialise in creating "cutting-edge radio imaging FX and sound design for radio, TV, and online." Little Monster Media is a great company and it was a pleasure, as always, to work with them. It's a big compliment when an audio production company asks you to do the voiceover work for their big advertisement and it was great to work with Adam and the others at Little Monster Media. I wish them all the best with this promotion!

Get in touch

Do you need my movie trailer voice for your next radio ad? If you do, I'd be more than happy to hear from you, so please feel free to get in touch. If you need to hear a little more of my voice before you make a commitment, I'll be more than happy to send over a sample of your script, free of charge.
