What is The Voice of God?

For many years I’ve been providing Voice of God announcements for major live events, concerts, award shows and brand experiences.

I’ve voiced shows for ITV, TV Choice, Butlins, Bestway, the National History Museum and more. I’m also the big “competition voice” on Britain’s Got Talent – a huge 2025 credit.

When The Masked Singer arrived at Butlins, they needed the full Voice of God treatment. I stepped in to bring the live show together.

I’ve also supplied the booming, festive Voice of God for large-scale Christmas events, including Santa-voiced fireworks displays where I voiced every announcement throughout the show.

Oh my… Voice of God – It’s The Big Weekend

My Voice of God delivery has been used on BBC Radio 1, Radio 2 and by top imaging teams for promos, trails and arena intros.

Event organisers also hire me for specialist roles, including:

If you’ve ever stayed at Butlins, there’s a high chance you’ve heard my Voice of God echoing through their venues.
