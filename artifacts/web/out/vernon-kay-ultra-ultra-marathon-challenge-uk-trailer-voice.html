British Trailer Voice for National BBC Campaign

When the BBC needed a strong, cinematic voice to promote Vernon Kay’s Ultra Ultra Marathon Challenge for Children in Need, they called me. My signature British movie trailer voice style gave the promo the dramatic weight it needed, broadcast across BBC Radio 2 all day, every day. It was bold, powerful and packed with energy – just like the challenge itself.

Children in Need – Vernon’s 115-Mile Ultra Challenge

Vernon Kay ran from Leicester to Bolton, covering over 115 miles in four days to raise money for BBC Children in Need. The promo followed his progress and inspired the public, helping raise a staggering £6 million. The trailer-style delivery brought a sense of scale and urgency to the challenge, turning heads across the UK.
