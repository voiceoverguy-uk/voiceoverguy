Pizza Hut Delivery Football Commentator

Back of the net for Pizza Hut! This national campaign was a brilliant chance to bring my football commentator voice style to one of the UK’s most recognisable takeaway brands.

June 2021

Pizza Hut Delivery Football Voice

2021 was packed with football campaigns, and Pizza Hut was one of the big ones. They wanted fast-paced, energetic commentary to match the excitement of their national delivery push. Alongside this gig, I also voiced the Just Eat UEFA campaign and another fun football-style spot for Manchester City FC.

I used my trusty MK416 mic for this one, delivering that big match feel Pizza Hut were after. If you’re looking for a football commentator voice for your own project, you’re in the right place.

Pizza Hut Delivery

We all know Pizza Hut, one of the world’s biggest and most recognisable takeaway brands. Founded in 1958, it has grown into a global favourite thanks to its classic pizzas, sides and family-friendly menus. With delivery now more convenient than ever, the brand continues to innovate its advertising, including using my football commentator voice style for high-energy promos.

If you liked this campaign, you might also enjoy my work for Just Eat UEFA or Manchester City, where the same energetic commentary style brings the brand message to life.

How do I get a football commentator voice?

Booking a football commentator-style voiceover is quick and easy. Just tell me what you need, where it will be used, and I’ll send a quote. Sessions can be recorded live via Source-Nexus or Cleanfeed, or I can record and deliver straight to your inbox.
