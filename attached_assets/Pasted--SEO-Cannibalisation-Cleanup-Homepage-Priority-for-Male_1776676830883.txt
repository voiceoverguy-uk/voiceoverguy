# SEO Cannibalisation Cleanup – Homepage Priority for "Male Voiceover"

Objective:
Refine a small group of existing pages so the homepage becomes the clearest and strongest ranking target for:
- male voiceover
- British male voiceover
- male voiceover artist
- UK male voiceover

Do NOT delete pages immediately.
Do NOT make reckless SEO changes.
Do NOT change any URL slugs in this task.
This is a controlled intent-cleanup and internal-linking task.

Context:
The homepage is the page we want Google to treat as the primary result for broad commercial queries such as:
- male voiceover
- British male voiceover
- male voiceover artist
- UK voice artist

Google is currently showing some cannibalisation / mixed intent across these pages:
- /voiceover-services-uk
- /why-use-voiceoverguy
- /english-voiceover

The site has recently been rebuilt and improved technically, so we do not want to overreact or remove pages without checking internal links and purpose first.

Important:
- Preserve the homepage as the main SEO target for broad "male voiceover" intent
- Reposition the other pages so they support the homepage instead of competing with it
- Keep British spelling
- Do not use em dashes
- Keep the tone polished, professional, human and natural
- No spammy keyword stuffing
- No unnecessary rewrites of unrelated pages
- Do not create redirect chains
- Do not implement any 301 redirects in this task unless explicitly listed in the final recommendations and only if the page clearly has no strategic value

Pages to audit and retarget:
1. /voiceover-services-uk
2. /why-use-voiceoverguy
3. /english-voiceover

What & Why:
Search Console suggests Google is testing multiple pages from the site for "male voiceover" related intent. We want to reduce overlap and strengthen the homepage as the dominant answer, while preserving useful pages that serve a distinct purpose.

Tasks:

1) Audit the 3 pages for cannibalisation risk
For each of the following pages:
- /voiceover-services-uk
- /why-use-voiceoverguy
- /english-voiceover

Review:
- title tag
- meta description
- H1
- opening paragraph
- subheadings
- repeated keyword phrases
- internal links pointing in and out
- whether the page is trying to target broad "male voiceover" intent
- whether the page has a distinct search purpose or is overlapping the homepage

2) Reposition each page to a clearer role

A. /voiceover-services-uk
This page currently risks competing directly with the homepage.
Retarget it away from broad "male voiceover" language and toward a broader service overview role.

Requirements:
- Replace any overly homepage-competing title such as "Hire a Professional Male Voiceover Artist"
- New title should focus on UK voiceover services / voiceover services in the UK / types of voiceover work
- H1 and opening copy should describe the page as a broader services overview, not the main male voiceover landing page
- Reduce use of exact phrases like:
  - male voiceover
  - British male voiceover
  - male voiceover artist
unless naturally necessary
- Add one sensible internal link to the homepage using natural anchor text such as:
  - British male voiceover artist
  - professional male voiceover
  - Guy Harris homepage
- Keep the page useful if it has value, but remove any sense that it is the main page for the broad target term

B. /why-use-voiceoverguy
Turn this into a trust / conversion support page, not a ranking competitor.

Requirements:
- Reframe the title, H1 and opening section around:
  - why clients choose Guy Harris
  - experience
  - reliability
  - studio quality
  - speed
  - credits
  - versatility
  - broadcast-quality audio
- This page should support conversion and trust, not target "male voiceover" as the primary phrase
- Remove or soften any repeated broad-match homepage keyword targeting
- Add a clear internal link back to the homepage with a natural anchor such as:
  - British male voiceover artist
  - Guy Harris
  - homepage
- Improve this page so it feels like a persuasive support page that helps both users and Google understand why Guy is trusted

C. /english-voiceover
This page should only exist if it has a clear purpose distinct from the homepage.
It should not compete for broad "male voiceover" intent.

Requirements:
- Rewrite title, H1 and opening content to focus tightly on "English voiceover" only
- Remove wording that makes it sound like an alternate homepage or broad male voiceover landing page
- Make sure the page clearly serves users looking specifically for an English voiceover artist or English voice style
- Add a natural supporting link to the homepage where appropriate
- If, after review, the page appears redundant, weak, or strategically unnecessary, do NOT delete it yet. Instead flag it in the final report as a possible future 301 redirect candidate after internal link checks and further monitoring

3) Internal link audit
Before changing anything structural, scan the site for internal links pointing to these three pages.

Check:
- header navigation
- footer navigation
- service pages
- blog posts
- homepage sections
- breadcrumbs
- CTAs
- sitemap references
- schema references if applicable

For each of the 3 pages, report:
- how many internal links point to it
- from which important pages
- whether those links should stay, be updated, or be reduced

4) Protect the homepage as the primary target
Review the homepage only lightly in this task.
Do NOT rewrite the homepage heavily.
Just confirm that it remains the clearest target for:
- male voiceover
- British male voiceover
- UK male voiceover artist

Check:
- title tag
- H1
- opening copy
- internal links from the three support pages back to the homepage

If a very small wording improvement is needed to reinforce intent, suggest it in the final report, but do not make major homepage changes in this task.

For each of the 3 pages, provide:

- suggested internal link anchor text back to the homepage

6) Final recommendation section
At the end, provide a short report with:
- Which page should clearly own "male voiceover" intent
