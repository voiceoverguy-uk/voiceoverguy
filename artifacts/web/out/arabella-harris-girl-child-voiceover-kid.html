Arabella Harris – British Girl Child Voiceover

Arabella Harris is a professional British child voiceover artist based in the UK. At just 9 years old, she’s already voiced national radio ads for Tesco, Sainsbury’s, KISS, Asda, Morrisons, Nickelodeon and more. With her natural, confident tone and clear British young girl voice, Arabella brings warmth, charm, and credibility to every script – whether it's for TV, radio, eLearning, or animation.

Looking to cast a talented child voice for your next campaign?

Projects & Experience:

Arabella’s voice has featured on UK radio, animated characters, and educational content aimed at children.
She’s also the voice of a Penguin attraction in Oman, proving her talent travels well beyond the UK. With clear diction, natural charm, and loves direction, she’s a favourite for fun, youthful reads.
Whether it’s playful, sincere, or full of excitement, her voice is perfect for capturing the imagination of young audiences, and often makes grown-ups smile too.
