The Voiceover Cartoons

As a voiceover for 16+ years, along the way, I’ve experienced a lot of situations and scenarios I’ve found amusing.
So, along with my unique sense of humour I hope I’ve captured various elements of what we do that will raise a smile.
Every day is different in this job yet there are similarities that make me smile daily. Hence making these Voiceover Cartoons.
To some viewers outside of the voiceover industry, most of these won’t mean anything and not that funny.
For those connected to this industry, then hopefully these voiceover cartoons should get an approving nod.
